Questions, answered.

Is the Google advertising budget included?

No. Advertising spend is paid to Google separately. Kenny Studio’s management fee is quoted independently, based on the agreed setup and ongoing work.

Can you guarantee enquiries or sales?

No. Outcomes depend on demand, competition, budget, offer, landing pages and your follow-up process. We report the data available and make informed adjustments without guaranteeing results.

What access is needed to get started?

We first review the website and campaign goals. Approved access may then be needed to your Google Ads, analytics and tag-management accounts. Do not send passwords; use account invitations and the permissions agreed for the project.
