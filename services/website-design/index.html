Questions, answered.

Can you work on an existing website?

Yes. Redesigns and improvements can be scoped after reviewing the current structure, platform, content and access. The right approach depends on what can be reused.

What determines the website quotation?

Page count, design complexity, content readiness, integrations, backend functionality and ongoing support affect the scope. Share your requirements for a tailored quote.

Does a website include ongoing SEO or advertising?

Search foundations can be included in the agreed build. Ongoing SEO and Google Ads management are separate services with their own scope. Advertising spend is separate from website or management fees.
