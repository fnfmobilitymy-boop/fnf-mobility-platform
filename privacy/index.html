KENNY STUDIO / PRIVACY

Your information and your choices.

The enquiry form prepares a WhatsApp message for you to review and send. This static website does not send the form through a website server. Opening WhatsApp takes you to a separate service.

The portfolio remembers language and music preferences on your device. Its legacy project-inquiry dialog stores a local draft only when you choose Save. You can remove these preferences and drafts through browser site-data settings.

Optional analytics is disabled unless a real account ID is configured and you choose to allow it. You can change your choice at any time. Google, WhatsApp, Telegram and linked portfolio sites have their own privacy practices. For privacy questions contact hello@kennystudio.cfd.
